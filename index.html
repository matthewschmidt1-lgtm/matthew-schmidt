The Three Lenses

Three Places To Look.

When you want to understand what's really happening, look at it three ways:

1) How does the business create value?
2) How does the system create leverage?
3) How do we help people do their best work?

The Path to Agency

The Path helps you see beneath the complexity.

Complexity becomes clearer when we learn to see the patterns and relationships that shape it. Knowledge becomes understanding, understanding becomes judgment, and judgment becomes agency.

The Friction

The problem usually isn't one of the three.

It's The Gap between them. The details change. The friction doesn't.

Business

Do you know what matters?

Everything gets measured. Not everyone agrees on what actually drives value.

System

Can the organization execute?

The strategy makes sense. The processes around it don't support it.

People

Can people act on it?

The answer exists. People don't have the clarity, authority, or incentive to act on it.
