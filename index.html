About

Twenty years at the intersection of data, consumers, and decisions.

Analytics→ CPG→ Leadership→ Tech→ Creativity

By day, I lead analytics as a director. Outside the org chart, I'm usually building an app, experimenting with an idea, or figuring out a better way to solve a problem.

I've worked across the beverage and CPG worlds, building an appreciation for what data can tell us — and what it can't. My curiosity doesn't stop when the workday ends: I build apps, experiment with technology, and chase ideas simply because I'm curious whether I can make them work.
