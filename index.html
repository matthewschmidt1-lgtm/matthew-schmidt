The Three Lenses

Three Places To Look.

Different books, different perspectives. But the same three questions keep showing up: How does the business create value? How does the system create leverage? And how do we help people do their best work?

Click a lens, or where circles overlap, to learn more.

The Friction

The problem usually isn't one of the three.

It's the gap between them. The details change. The friction doesn't.

Business

Do you know what matters?

Everything gets measured. Not everyone agrees on what actually drives value.

System

Can the organization execute?

The strategy makes sense. The processes around it don't support it.

People

Can people act on it?

The answer exists. People don't have the clarity, authority, or incentive to act on it.

The interesting problems rarely live inside just one of these, they live in the gap between them. That's usually where I start.
